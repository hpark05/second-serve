Suites

Unit 1

2650 Durant Avenue (Located one block from the south side of campus)

The Christian and Slottman halls offer mini-suites. Each building can accommodate up to 170 guests in double-occupancy rooms. Each mini-suite consists of two spacious bedrooms, either two double-occupancy bedrooms or one double-occupancy bedroom and one triple-occupancy bedroom, plus its own bathroom.

View 3D Room Layout

Unit 2

2650 Haste Street (Located 3 blocks from the south side of campus)

Towle hall offers mini-suites. The building can accommodate up to 180 guests in double-occupancy rooms. Each mini-suite consists of two spacious bedrooms, either two double-occupancy bedrooms or one double-occupancy bedroom and one triple-occupancy bedroom, plus its own bathroom.

View 3D Room Layout

Foothill 

2700 Hearst Avenue (Located on the northeast side of campus)

Foothill is nestled in a eucalyptus grove and was designed to blend with the natural beauty of the Berkeley hills. Wood shingles, mature trees, and redwood decks are just a few of the unique features. 

The complex houses up to 700 guests. Accommodations are provided in suite arrangements varying from two to 11 double-occupancy bedrooms. All furnishings are solid oak and each suite has a separate entrance, living room, and shared bathroom(s).

View 3D Room Layout

Clark Kerr Suites

2601 Warring Street (Located six blocks southeast of the main campus)

For meeting planners who are seeking a more historical event experience, this beautiful Spanish-style complex, highlighted by terra cotta accents, tiled fountains, and landscaped courtyards, is a self-contained venue offering meeting space, lodging, and dining services.

Clark Kerr Suites can accommodate up to 250 guests in double-occupancy rooms. The two- and three-bedroom suites have attractive living rooms and one or two bathrooms that are shared by the occupants of the suite. Each bedroom may be locked to ensure privacy.
