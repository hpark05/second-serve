Units 1, 2, and 3 Residence Halls
Units 1, 2, and 3 Residence Halls
Units 1, 2, and 3 are three large complexes consisting of several high rise residence hall towers, each built around a central courtyard. Shown here is a Unit 1 residence hall tower.
Units 1, 2, and 3 are located a few blocks from the south side of campus. Shown here is Towle Hall in Unit 2.
All the Units are close to local cafes, restaurants, and shops. Shown here is one of the six buildings that share a central courtyard as part of Unit 1.
Paved central courtyards with trees and lawns provide shaded and sunny outdoor spaces. This view overlooks the central courtyard from one of Unit 2’s high-rise residence towers.
Unit 3 has four high-rise buildings around a large central courtyard. Two of the residence buildings face Durant Avenue.
Triple rooms are the most common room types in the Units and typically have a loft bed and bunk bed. Double rooms and a limited number of single rooms are available.
Mini-suites are a room type available in Units 1 and 2. Mini-suites have two spacious shared bedrooms and one semi-private shared bathroom.
Community bathrooms are available on each floor. Single-gender and all-gender bathrooms are available on different floors.
Lounges throughout the units give students space to study and hang out with friends.
Amenities in residence halls include a communal laundry center, mail services, and bike storage on site.
Units 1 and 2 are only half a block away from Crossroads Dining Commons. Residence halls include the Blue and Gold Meal Plan, with upgrades and add-ons available.
Unit 3 has its own dining commons, Café 3, located in a building in the central courtyard. Enjoy relaxed, lounge-feeling dining with chill vibes. Bear Market campus convenience store is also located in Unit 3.

Units 1, 2, and 3 are high-rise residence hall complexes, each with multiple buildings arranged around a large central courtyard. Located in a lively, urban neighborhood near campus. Convenient dining commons, food options, shops, and downtown Berkeley are all located at your doorstep. Offers a dynamic social environment in traditional corridor-style room layouts, with some mini-suites in Units 1 and 2.
Location
Unit 1: One block south of campus at
2650 Durant Avenue
.
Unit 2: Three blocks south of campus at
2650 Haste Street
.
Unit 3: One block south of campus at
2400 Durant Avenue
.
Close to Sproul Plaza, Student Union MLK Jr. Building, and the Student Center.
Room types
Traditional corridor-style residence hall:
Rooms along a central hallway that share a communal bathroom and lounges. Most floors are all gender; single-gender floors are also available. Single-gender floors have single-gender community bathrooms.
Residence Hall Mini-Suites
: A unit consisting of two bedrooms — often a mix of double- and triple-occupancy — that share a private, dedicated bathroom.
Room occupancy
Double, triple, and a limited number of single-occupancy bedrooms are available.
Each high-rise building has eight floors of double and triple-occupancy rooms.
General note: There are very few single rooms at UC Berkeley, and they are typically reserved for students with a
documented disability need
.
Nearest dining options
Units 1 and 2:
Crossroads dining commons
is only a half-block away.
On-site at Unit 3:
Café 3 dining commons
is located on-site.
Convenience store:
Bear Market
is located in Unit 3.
Special features
Some rooms offer panoramic views of the Bay or the Berkeley hills.
Main lounge on entry level; additional lounges on upper floors.
All units have an on-site
Academic Center
with flexible study spaces.
Unit 1 and 2: A central building houses the unit office, mail services, and rooms for meetings and events.
Unit 3: The central commons building houses Café 3, Bear Market, the unit office, mail services, rooms for meetings, and events.
Unit 1:

Freeborn Hall, substance-free environment:
Residents actively agree to keep the community free of smoke, alcohol and drugs. Hall staff and students plan and host substance-free programs, events, and activities.
