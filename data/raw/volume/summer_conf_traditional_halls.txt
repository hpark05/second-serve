Traditional Residence Halls
Unit 1, 2, and 3
Accommodations are available in our high-rise complex of four buildings. Each building houses approximately 200 guests in double-occupancy rooms.
Each room features a single bed, chest of drawers, desk with chair, closet, mirror, wastebasket, bookshelf, and wall lights. Each floor has one central bathroom with private toilets, shower stalls, and washbasins.

Stern
2700 Hearst Avenue
(Located near the northeast side of campus)
The Stern housing building is nestled in a eucalyptus grove and was designed to blend with the natural beauty of the Berkeley hills. Wood shingles, mature trees, and redwood decks are just a few of the unique features.
The complex houses up to 300 guests in double-occupancy rooms. Each room features a single bed, chest of drawers, desk with chair, closet, mirror, wastebasket, bookshelf, and wall lights. Each floor has one central bathroom with private toilets, shower stalls, and washbasins.
View 3D Room Layout
Clark Kerr Campus
2601 Warring Street
(Located six blocks southeast of the main campus)
For meeting planners who are seeking a more historical event experience, this beautiful Spanish-style complex, highlighted by terra cotta accents, tiled fountains, and landscaped courtyards, is a self-contained venue offering meeting space, lodging, and dining services.
The complex houses up to 400 guests in double-occupancy rooms. Each room features a single bed, chest of drawers, desk with chair, closet, mirror, wastebasket, bookshelf, and wall lights.  Each floor has one central bathroom with private toilets, shower stalls, and washbasins.
