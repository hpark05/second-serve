How overpriced is Cal Dining?
By Sherdil Niyaz
Feb 4, 2014
Feb 4, 2014

People always seem to complain about how expensive groceries and other foods bought on campus are. Many students are even willing to make the long and dangerous trek to far-away supermarkets in hopes of saving some real cash, but the question is: Is it worth it? How much of your booze money are you actually saving by going to Trader Joe's instead of places like GBC to get your cereal? In true Clog fashion, we present to you a quick-and-dirty list of four random items we found at Foothill's Cub Market ... and their prices on Safeway's website for comparison.
Frosted Flakes
(10.5 oz)
Price at Cub Market: $5.95 Price at Safeway: $4.99 Cal Dining Markup:
19%
Naked Fruit Drink (15.2 oz)
Price at Cub Market: $3.50 Price at Safeway: $2.50 Cal Dining Markup:
40%
Pepperidge Farms Milano Cookies
Price at Cub Market: $5.95 Price at Safeway: $4.39 Cal Dining Markup:
35.5%
Pepsi (20 oz)
Price at Cub Market: $1.90 Price at Safeway: $1.89 Cal Dining Markup:
0%
The results are surprising (Who knew that Pepsi from Cub Market was actually cheap?), but also show a definite markup for some items. Keep in mind that you have to factor in price change over time as well. The bottom line is that it pays to do your homework and shop around ... before you shop around. So why is everything so expensive (except that Pepsi)? Most people would probably say its because of convenience — those Frosted Flakes are available right at your doorstep, and that's great (get it?). Surely you'd be expected to pay a premium for that kind of convenience. The fact that the university is strapped for cash doesn't really help either. Of course, you could take the conspiracy-happy (aka more fun) theory that Cal Dining is trying to screw everybody by creating a monopoly. And that just as well might be true — after all, where else can you buy common food items on campus? In the end, though, you still have to factor in other costs as well. Taking a bus to go get food costs money and time. In that sense, you could also paint Cal Dining as giving you a choice (see how open ended this is?). Ultimately, it's up to you how much is too much. It's also a good time to note that your mileage may vary. There was a wide variance in markups for different items, so keep that in mind. We're just here to inform, folks. Now pardon us while we enjoy those Milanos we were forced to buy for "research" purposes. Image Source:
