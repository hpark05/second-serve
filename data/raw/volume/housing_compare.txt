Compare Housing Options
Use our Location Filter to compare UC Berkeley Housing’s wide variety of residence hall and apartment communities. Whether you’re looking for a lively urban environment, neighborhood close to nature, or a space for your family, there are communities to fit every type of student.
UC Berkeley Housing Location Filter
The Location Filter can help you explore housing features and locations. Explore your options based on your resident type and housing needs.
Preference tip:
Remember to pick “any room size, any location” as one of your housing preferences to improve your chances of receiving an offer. Undergraduates who qualify for the
Housing Guarantee
must pick “any room size, any location” to maintain eligibility for the guarantee.
Note for undergraduates:
First-year undergraduate students will usually be offered a triple residence hall room. There are very few undergraduate single rooms at UC Berkeley, and they are typically reserved for students with a
documented disability need
.
