Residence Halls
Living on campus is one of the best ways to experience UC Berkeley. Our residence halls offer more than just a place to sleep: they provide an engaging, supportive community where you can connect, grow, and thrive. From making lifelong friendships to easy access to classes and campus resources, your residence hall is the heart of your college experience.
What do residence halls offer?
All-inclusive amenities:
Includes all utilities, Wi-Fi, and community spaces. Rooms are
fully furnished
, including a microchill refrigerator and microwave combo.
On-site conveniences:
Each location has a laundry center, mail services, recreation room, and bike storage. Most locations include a fitness center or access to one nearby.
Prime locations:
Close to campus, offering easy access to academic buildings, libraries, dining options, and campus facilities.
Live-in support, 24/7:
Dedicated live-in staff support, from resident advisors (RAs) to faculty in residence.
Academic centers:
All residents have access to
academic services centers
(with tutoring, exam review sessions, and more) at five of our residence halls.
Secure access:
Each building has secured entry with trained
residential safety staff
and 24/7 support.
Residence hall options
Room types:
Traditional residence halls, suites, and mini-suites.
Traditional corridor-style residence hall: Rooms along a central hallway that share a communal bathroom and lounges.
Suites: Collection of rooms that share bathrooms, a common area, and a separate locked entrance.
Mini-suites: A unit with two, multi-occupancy bedrooms that share a private, dedicated bathroom.
Room occupancy:
While triples are our most common option, we offer several room configurations. General Note: There are very few single rooms, and they are typically reserved for students with a documented disability need.
UC Berkeley residence halls
Blackwell Residence Hall
Modern, eight-story residence hall offering premium double rooms with no bunk beds. Traditional corridor-style room layouts. Upscale amenities include an on-site fitness center, hotel-like lobby, and spacious study lounges at a prime location one block from campus and the Recreational Sports Facility.
Clark Kerr Residence Hall
Traditional corridor-style and suite-style rooms located in a low-rise mini-neighborhood six blocks from campus. This Spanish mission-style complex offers tree-lined courtyards, green spaces, a historic dining commons, and access to nature trails, pools, and an outdoor track. The neighborhood is ideal for students looking for a quieter, less urban environment.
Foothill Residence Hall
Suite-style rooms in low-rise wood-shingled buildings surrounded by tall trees overlooking courtyards and the Bay. A peaceful housing complex nestled near the Berkeley hills and conveniently close to STEM classrooms, green spaces, and nature trails. Ideal for students looking for a quieter, less urban environment. Foothill’s newly renovated dining commons provides allergen-friendly dining options in ...
Martinez Commons Residence Hall
Premium, modern, eco-friendly residence hall located two blocks from campus. Amenities include an on-site fitness center, hotel-like lobby, interior courtyard, spacious study lounges, and a prime location close to cafés, shops, and campus life. Martinez Commons offers traditional corridor-style room layouts (mostly double rooms without bunk beds) and is the only residence hall open over ...
Stern Residence Hall
Welcomes womxn-identified students in spacious, traditional corridor-style and suite-style rooms. Located in low-rise buildings next to the Berkeley Hills adjacent to the Foothill complex and sharing the same dining commons. Offers quieter surroundings with easy access to STEM buildings, hiking trails, and stunning views of the Bay. Ideal for students looking for a quieter, less ...
Units 1, 2, and 3 Residence Halls
High-rise residence hall complexes, each with multiple buildings arranged around a large central courtyard. Located in a lively, urban neighborhood near campus. Convenient dining commons, food options, shops, and downtown Berkeley are all located at your doorstep. Offers both a dynamic social environment in traditional corridor-style room layouts, and mini-suites in Units 1 and 2.
Note: Blackwell Hall is an affiliated property assigned by the university. On-site management is overseen by public-private partners.
