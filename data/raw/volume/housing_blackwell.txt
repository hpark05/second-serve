Blackwell Residence Hall
Blackwell Residence Hall
Blackwell Residence Hall is a modern, eight-story residence hall with premium double rooms: two residents per room with no bunk beds. Upscale amenities include an expansive on-site fitness center, a hotel-like lobby, spacious study lounges, and a prime location one block from campus and the Recreational Sports Facility.
The newest residence hall at UC Berkeley, Blackwell Hall is a large eight-story building, located one block from campus.
The lobby of Blackwell Hall. Students can pick up packages and mail, hang out with friends, and study.
The lobby is home to a pool table and a ping pong table.
The fitness center in Blackwell Hall is equipped with treadmills, ellipticals, and weights.
The academic center in Blackwell Hall has access to computers, printers, and study spaces.
Every floor of Blackwell Hall has a study lounge.
Blackwell Hall is made up for mostly double rooms.

Blackwell Hall is a modern, eight-story residence hall offering premium double rooms with no bunk beds. Upscale amenities include an on-site fitness center, hotel-like lobby, and spacious study lounges at a prime location one block from campus and the Recreational Sports Facility.
Location
One block from the south side of campus at
2401 Durant Ave
.
Close to shops, restaurants, the
Recreational Sports Facility
, and Sproul Plaza.
Room types
Premium corridor-style residence hall:
Blackwell is a premium residence hall featuring larger-than-standard rooms and no bunk beds. Rooms are along a central hallway that share communal bathrooms and lounges.
Most floors are all gender with multiple all-gender bathrooms per floor.
Rooms are furnished. For details, visit
Furnishings in My Room
.
Room occupancy
Double-occupancy rooms and a limited number of single-occupancy rooms are available.
General note: There are very few single rooms at UC Berkeley, and they are typically reserved for students with a
documented disability need
.
Nearest dining options
Café 3 dining commons
is only a block away at 2400 Durant Avenue.
Bear Market
convenience store is next to Café 3.
Special features
UC Berkeley’s newest residence hall.
Eight-story high-rise only one block from campus.
Features an expansive on-site fitness center with ellipticals, treadmills, and weights.
Lounges and study rooms available on each floor.
About the building
Named after
David Blackwell
, the first Black professor ever to receive tenure at UC Berkeley and a preeminent statistician who made the campus his home for almost 50 years.

Affiliate housing:
Blackwell Hall is an affiliated property classified as on-campus housing for financial aid. On-site management is overseen by public-private partners. Affiliated properties may have different on-site amenities and resources than university-managed properties based on facilities available.
