Off-Campus Meal Plans 2026–2027
Undergraduate students living in off-campus housing or university-owned/affiliated apartments, as well as graduate/extension students.
Balancing living and learning is easier with a meal plan from UC Berkeley Dining. Off-campus meal plans are available by semester for flexibility, convenience, and value. Choose the plan that best fits your needs.

How meal plans work
Off-campus meal plans may include one or both of the following components, depending on the plan selected:
Meal swipes:
A set number of meal swipes to use each week at the dining commons or as a meal swipe equivalency at participating locations.
Meal swipes reset weekly. Any unused swipes expire on Saturday at midnight and do not roll over to the following week.
Meal swipes may only be used by the meal plan holder. Meal swipes are non-transferable and may not be used to pay for guest meals.
Meal swipes and meal swipe equivalencies are subject to usage limits.
Flex and flex+ dollars:
A set number of flex dollars and flex+ dollars are available each semester and function like cash on a declining balance.
Flex dollars: Use at all UC Berkeley Dining locations, including UC Berkeley Dining pickup on Grubhub.
Flex+ dollars: Use everywhere flex dollars do, plus Grubhub nationwide.
Unused balances of flex dollars, flex+ dollars, and add-on flex+ dollars roll over from fall to spring and expire at the end of the spring semester and do not roll over into summer sessions or the next academic year.
UC Berkeley Dining maximizes your time on campus and minimizes the hassle of what to eat with a variety of meal plans to choose from. Select from flexible plans with meal swipes, flex dollars, and flex+ dollars, or choose from simple plans with flex and flex+ dollars only. The most popular meal plan is the Advantage Plan, which is simple, flexible, and budget-friendly.
