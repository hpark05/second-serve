Clark Kerr
Clark Kerr
2601 Warring Street
Check for hours
Home to four hot food stations, and a salad and deli bar, Clark Kerr dining commons is a relaxed, welcoming location with a Hogwarts-like atmosphere. Enjoy a great variety of options: enchiladas, beef stew, or Tikka Masala; made-to-order pizza and pasta with various sauces; Halal beef hamburger or vegan patties; vegan and vegetarian items like grilled polenta, white bean ragout, and wild rice stuffed peppers. The allergy-friendly station is free of the nine major allergens and offers Halal chicken and beef, and gluten-free items. Our family-oriented staff serves about 1,200 students every day.
Recommendation: Dessert (vegan options too!)
