Crossroads
2415 Bowditch Street
Check for hours
As the flagship of Berkeley Dining, Crossroads dining commons has a modern vibe and is well known for great flavor profiles and international foods. Its 10 stations serve a variety of eats like pho, pizza, deli, salad bar, and more — all with close attention to food labels, allergen-free platforms, and dietary needs. Discover right next door
The Den
, featuring
Pizzeria 1868
and Peet’s Coffee. The diverse, family-oriented team welcomes 4,400 students through its doors every day.
Recommendation: Lemon Grass (Pho)
