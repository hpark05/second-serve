Dining Commons
UC Berkeley Dining has four all-you-care-to-eat dining commons across campus. Wherever you stop in, each offers a wide array of healthy, great-tasting meals.
Café 3
Located inside Unit 3 residence hall
Clark Kerr
Located inside Clark Kerr residence hall
Crossroads
Located between Unit 1 and Unit 2 residence halls
Foothill
Located inside Foothill residence hall
